This Charter governs the conduct, discipline, and soul of every operative assigned to the Red Right Hand. Violation of these articles results in immediate termination.

h2(#article-i-the-law-of-anonymity). ARTICLE I: THE LAW OF ANONYMITY

bq. "The Hand Has No Name."

From the moment of induction from the Navy, Army, or Marine staging grounds on Corin, the operative ceases to exist as an individual. You have no past. You have no future outside the unit. You are a callsign and a weapon hidden inside a routine naval logistics shell. You will seek no glory, claim no credit, and leave no trace. To be known is to fail.

h2(#article-ii-the-law-of-absolute-resolve). ARTICLE II: THE LAW OF ABSOLUTE RESOLVE

bq. "The Grip Does Not Yield."

Once an objective is assigned by the Tribunal, it must be completed. There is no retreat, no surrender, and no failure. Combining naval precision striking power with heavy ground dominance, we treat obstacles as simple variables to be eliminated. The mission ends only when the target is neutralized or the operative is dead.

h2(#article-iii-the-law-of-deniability). ARTICLE III: THE LAW OF DENIABILITY

bq. "Blood Washes Off."

If captured or killed, you are alone. The UEE will not acknowledge you, and the Tribunal will not negotiate for you. In the event of death, your record is retroactively scrubbed, and your fatality is logged as a routine transport accident or pirate ambush under standard 174th Battle Group logistics duties. You carry the means to ensure you are never interrogated. You will protect the silence with your final breath.

h2(#article-iv-the-law-of-brotherhood). ARTICLE IV: THE LAW OF BROTHERHOOD

bq. "One Pulse, One Vein."

Internal conflict is the death of the unit. Loyalty to the operative beside you is absolute, second only to the mission. We share the weight of the blood we spill. No operative is left behind, dead or alive, unless their recovery directly compromises the existence of the unit or the New Tribunal itself.

h2(#article-v-the-law-of-retribution). ARTICLE V: THE LAW OF RETRIBUTION

bq. "Dextera Sanguinea Vindictae."

We do not strike in anger, but we strike with devastating finality. Operating with the relentless precision of elite Advocacy ghost-agents, violence is our currency, and we pay in full. When we are unleashed, we ensure that the threat is not just stopped, but dismantled so thoroughly that it can never rise again.
