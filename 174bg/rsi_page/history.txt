h2. COMPROMISED DOSSIER: OPERATIONAL OVERVIEW

*PUBLIC DESIGNATION:* 174th Battle Group
*INTERNAL CODENAME:* The Red Right Hand
*COMMAND AUTHORITY:* The New Tribunal (Classified)
*PUBLIC COVER:* Logistics & Support Division, UEE Navy

h3. Historical Genesis

The birth of the Red Right Hand stems from a growing conflict between political idealism and cosmic realities within the contemporary United Empire of Earth (UEE). Following the fall of the fascist Messer Era, the UEE Senate became consumed with making public restitution for centuries of authoritarian tyranny. This pivot produced a government fixated on radical transparency and procedural ethics.

However, the galaxy did not soften alongside human politics. Relentless Vanduul raids, deep-space piracy, and sprawling criminal syndicates continued to threaten human expansion. To bypass political paralysis, a shadow executive council emerged: *The New Tribunal*.

Deliberately echoing the historical 2523 United Planets of Earth (UPE) Tribunal, originally governed by a High-Secretary, High-General, and High-Advocate, this clandestine body operates entirely off the grid. To enforce its will, the New Tribunal required a flexible, unacknowledged force capable of executing high-risk black operations, from extra-jurisdictional extractions to unsanctioned strikes deep within Xi'An space.

h3. The Public Face: 174th Battle Group

To conceal its lethal intent, the strike force operates under the cover of the *174th Battle Group*, publicly documented as an unremarkable logistics and transport element within the UEE Navy.

* *Hiding in Plain Sight:* As the largest military branch, the UEE Navy moves millions of tons of material across dozens of star systems daily. Disguising a strike team within this massive logistical machine provides perfect operational camouflage.

* *Recruitment Pipeline:* Operatives are discreetly hand-picked from Army and Navy candidate pools, as well as battle-hardened Marines stationed on the frigid planet Corin in the Kilian System.

* *Fiscal Cover:* The government's current mandate to "do more with less" under tight military budgets creates an ideal environment for off-the-books requisitions and black-budget allocations to vanish without raising alarms.

h3. The Inner Circle: The Red Right Hand

Internally, the unit is known only as *The Red Right Hand*.

While the Advocacy handles civil counterespionage, internal affairs, and high-profile fugitives, the Red Right Hand functions as the Tribunal's lethal military hammer. They operate with the terrifying efficiency of top-tier Advocacy agents, whose methodology relies on relentlessly pursuing targets until they are permanently "ghosted" (eliminated).

h4. Key Operational Characteristics

* *Combined Arms Integration:* Melds the sustained ground dominance of the mechanized UEE Army with the swift, surgical deployment capabilities of the Navy.

* *Absolute Deniability:* Missions do not officially exist. If a Red Right Hand operative is killed in action, their record is retroactively scrubbed, and their casualty report is logged as a routine workplace accident or pirate ambush during standard 174th transport duties.
